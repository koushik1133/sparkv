04 FAQ

Straight answers.

How much does a project cost and how long does it take?

It depends on scope, integrations, security and rollout needs, so SparkV does not publish fixed prices or timelines. Describe the problem, the current workflow and the outcome you want, and SparkV will respond with a focused technical path forward.

How do I start a project with SparkV?

Use the project form or email us. Share what you want to build, automate or improve, including the current workflow and what success looks like.

Do AI agents act without human oversight?

No. Agents are designed to research, support, qualify, coordinate and execute with human oversight.

Can you connect to the tools we already use?

Yes. Integration is a dedicated step: data, APIs, AI models and the tools your team already relies on.
